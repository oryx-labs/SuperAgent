# Why SuperAgent

**AI can do the work now, but companies don't have a place for AI to do it.** Employees use AI on their own: their own tokens, their own agents, their own laptops. The company needs one system that gathers that use up, so it is controlled, kept and paid for well.

## What we believe

1. **Scattered AI use will be gathered up by companies.** Every technology that enters a company starts with individuals and ends with the company managing it. AI is no different. The lasting value of the system that does this is **control, accumulation and cost**.
2. **Leave the intelligence to the models and official agents.** Calling model APIs and building our own agent loop could never keep up with official agents, which are tuned together with their models and improve every week. So SuperAgent doesn't rebuild them: it drives the official agent CLIs directly. Every improvement in models and agents becomes an improvement in SuperAgent.
3. **The value is at the company level.** However capable an agent is, it does one job well in one session for one person. A company needs more: who owns the results, who can see them, how work is handed over, who does what, what it costs, which systems it touches. These span people, time and systems, and only a company-wide system can carry them. SuperAgent helps companies **use AI well, cut cost and get more done**.
4. **Stay neutral about model vendors.** Any vendor's models can be used, along with a company's own model gateway and self-hosted models. Each task goes to the model that fits it: the hardest work to the strongest model, everyday work to one that is good and cheap. The model landscape changes fast; neutrality means a company always uses what's best right now, and what it builds up belongs to the company, not to any model.
5. **Agents must follow the rules before companies let them work.** An agent can run any command and touch any file. That's its power, and the reason companies hesitate. SuperAgent keeps each agent isolated, holds the credentials centrally, records what it does, and brings the important decisions back to people. **What can be controlled can be trusted with more.**
6. **The more complex the work, the more it needs a system to run it.** Tasks grow from minutes to hours, from one person to a team, from once to every day. Companies need something to **assign, schedule, track and run** them so they finish reliably and cheaply.

## Who it's for

SuperAgent is built for **companies**, and used by everyone in them.

| Who | What they care about | What SuperAgent gives them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employees | Easy to use, gets the work done | An AI team from their first day: say what's needed, and it gets broken down, done and followed up |
| Team leads | Moving together, no gaps when people change | Work around the same agent; good methods become team skills; a successor inherits an agent that knows the work |
| Company leaders | Efficiency, cost, risk, and what the company keeps | Audit, analytics and control over AI across the company; cost optimized by the system; assets that keep growing |
| IT and security | Deployment, permissions, data, compliance | Deploy once for everyone; central keys and authorization; isolated, recorded agents; self-hosted |

The first companies to need it do a lot of knowledge work, already have employees using AI on their own, and want to bring it under control and spread it further.

## Where it's going

**Automated, intelligent, always on.** The same people do more; the same work needs fewer people.

Today, SuperAgent helps each person and AI finish work together. As models get stronger, the software matures and each company's knowledge builds up, people need to step in less and less:

1. **People work with AI.** People are involved at every step.
2. **AI finishes, people confirm key points.** Common work is done by AI on its own; people step in before delivery and at real decisions.
3. **Processes run, people check results.** Recurring work becomes automation; people handle the exceptions.
4. **Always on, people set direction.** The system keeps taking work in and delivering it; people own the goals and the decisions that matter.

![Over time, agents finish more work on their own, people step in less, and each job costs less](/diagrams/product-automation-trend-en.jpg)

Every file, memory and skill kept today is fuel for tomorrow's automation. The aim always lands on the work itself: SuperAgent is a place to get work done, not a platform for building AI.
