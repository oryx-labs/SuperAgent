# Why SuperAgent

AI is no longer optional for companies. What they need is AI that **actually gets used**: it's useful, it's affordable, and it can take on any kind of work. Handing every employee a personal assistant doesn't get there.

## What goes wrong with personal assistants

- **The work leaves with the person.** Conversations, context, and methods sit in personal accounts. When someone changes roles or leaves, the company starts over.
- **Nobody builds on anyone else.** Colleagues can't work with the same AI on the same job, and a successor can't continue a predecessor's work.
- **Knowledge doesn't accumulate.** What one person figures out never becomes something the team or company can use.
- **Cost is scattered and wasteful.** Employees subscribe on their own (or don't get AI at all), and everyone uses the most expensive model for everything.
- **The company is blind.** There is no audit, no security boundary, and no connection to approvals.

## What SuperAgent does instead

- **One system for everyone.** All work happens in the company's system, so it becomes company assets, and the system distills it into knowledge.
- **Collaboration and continuity.** People share agents on the same job; when someone leaves, their agents and context are handed over.
- **Assets at every level.** Skills and knowledge for each employee, each team, and the whole company.
- **AI on day one, paid by usage.** No personal subscriptions; the system bills by usage and routes each task to a model that fits its difficulty.
- **Visible and safe.** Built-in audit and security, with office tools and approvals connected.

## The result

**Company-controlled. Cost-efficient. Accumulates assets automatically. Easy to use.**
