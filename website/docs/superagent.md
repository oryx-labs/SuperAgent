# SuperAgent

Every employee has a **SuperAgent**: a lead that turns what you drop in into work done by a small team of agents. You don't have to wait for it; send as many things as you like.

## How it works

1. **Drop it in.** Send thoughts, requests or files whenever you like. They go into its inbox.
2. **It plans.** It works out what you want and proposes a plan. **Nothing starts until you approve it.**
3. **It forms a team.** It hires agents for the work (with the right persona and skills), or uses ones it already has.
4. **It hands out the work.** It assigns tasks to team members, or starts a task directly for something simple.
5. **It reports back.** Progress and results come back to you, and finished work shows on your [to-do](./agents#to-do) page.

## What it can do for you

- **Team** — hire members, change their setup, see what each is working on. Hiring or changing members always goes through a plan you approve.
- **Tasks** — start tasks, assign them, follow them, answer when a task asks for a decision.
- **Files** — find, read, move, rename, tag, organize into folders and delete your files, so you can just ask it to tidy up.
- **Skills** — list the company's and your skills, and use them when hiring.
- **Collaboration** — find colleagues and propose inviting them to an agent; the owner confirms.

## Chat and background

Your conversation with the SuperAgent stays a conversation. What it does on its own (handling messages from outside, following up on tasks) runs in the background and only posts into your chat when there's something to tell you.

## Messages from outside

When the company's [Feishu app](./connectors#feishu) is on, your messages flow to your SuperAgent:

<Shot name="message-stream" alt="The message stream: each message and what SuperAgent did with it" />

- It sorts them: **urgent** ones right away (and it can ping you in Feishu), **important** ones every hour, the rest in a **daily digest**.
- Sorting needs no setup: it uses the org chart (your manager and direct reports) and a fixed set of urgent signals.
- For each message it decides what to do: tell you, add a to-do, propose a plan for work the team can take on, or just note it.
- **Safety:** while handling outside messages it can only use read-only and inbox tools. It can't hire, assign or act on someone else's words; anything that acts goes through a plan you approve.

The **message stream** shows every message and what your SuperAgent did with it. Open it from the SuperAgent's details.
